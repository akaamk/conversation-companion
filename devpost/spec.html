Conversation Companion

Three web pages, the browser's built-in voice, and one small helper on Vercel that talks to Claude Haiku 5.5.

Status: approved

How it works, in plain language

The pages are plain HTML, with CSS for the look and JavaScript to make them do things. No framework.

The questionnaire keeps the family's answers in the browser for that visit only, like a sticky note that disappears when the tab is closed.

The conversation page uses Chrome or Edge's built-in speech recognition to turn the elder's words into text, and speech synthesis to read replies aloud.

The helper is a tiny program on Vercel that wakes up when asked. It's the only part that holds your secret Anthropic key. It turns the answers into companion instructions, asks Claude for a reply, and sends the reply back.

Components

Page Home → prd: Home Page
index.html. Your welcome copy and a Get started link.
Page Questionnaire → prd: Family Questionnaire
questionnaire.html + js/questionnaire.js. Eight large text boxes, with the name required. On submit, answers are saved to session storage and the page moves on.
Page Conversation → prd: Voice Conversation
  • Listening: continuous recognition. A turn ends after about 2.5 seconds of quiet, so pauses mid-story don't cut the elder off.
  • Speaking: a natural-sounding voice where available, at a slightly slower pace. The microphone is paused while the companion talks.
  • Orb states: a slow breathing glow while listening, a dim pulse while thinking, a brighter pulse while speaking.
  • Ending: Claude adds a hidden [END] after a goodbye. After about 45 seconds of quiet it asks "Are you still there?", and after about 20 more seconds it says goodbye and stops.
Helper Chat helper → prd: Tailored Companion
api/chat.js. Receives the answers and the conversation so far, builds the instructions, calls Claude with low effort and short replies, and returns the reply. If Claude declines or something fails, it sends back a gentle fallback line.
Kernel Companion instructions → prd: Tailored Companion
api/companion-instructions.js. Your GPT rules, made general, plus "About the person" from the answers, plus a marked "Know but do not mention first" section from question 6.

Where it could break

Risk Mishears or hears nothing
Gently: "I didn't quite catch that. Could you say it again?"
Risk Microphone blocked or wrong browser
An on-screen note for the family member explaining how to allow the microphone, or to use Chrome or Edge.
Risk Claude or the helper fails
The orb stops, a message appears for the family member, and the companion says "I'm sorry, I need to stop for now."
Risk Replies feel slow
The thinking glow shows it's working. If it's still too slow, add sentence-by-sentence streaming.
